ADSR Envelope Simulator

Objective: Experiment visually and audibly with the four stages of the ADSR envelope (Attack, Decay, Sustain, Release) to sculpt a synthesizer's volume over time.

Instructions & Accessibility:

  1. Click Start Simulator to begin.
  2. Press and hold the Hold Note button (or hold the Spacebar) to trigger the sound, and release it to stop.
  3. Crucial: Adjust the sliders before holding the note. Changing parameters while holding the button may cause audio calculation delays.
  4. Complete the 5 sequential missions to learn how to sculpt percussive and sustained profiles.
