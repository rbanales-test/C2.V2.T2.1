ADSR Envelope Simulator

Objective: Experiment visually and audibly with the four stages of the ADSR envelope (Attack, Decay, Sustain, Release) to sculpt a synthesizer's volume over time.

Instructions:

  1. Click Start Simulator to begin.
  2. Press and hold the Hold Note button to trigger the sound, and release it to stop.
  3. Complete the 5 sequential missions to learn how to sculpt percussive and sustained profiles.
  4. The canvas will draw your envelope shape dynamically.
